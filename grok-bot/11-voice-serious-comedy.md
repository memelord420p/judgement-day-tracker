# Brand voice — serious + comedy

Judgment Day Tracker covers **both**:

1. **Serious** — agentic cyber attacks, autonomous weapons, injuries, policy, real capability leaps  
2. **Comedy** — Robot Olympics faceplants, wrong-way robots, victory dances, uncanny fails, spectacular wins  

Same deadpan terminal voice. Never punch down on injured people; safety incidents stay sober. Viral fails can headline without tanking scores unless they reveal a real capability/safety shift.

B) COMEDY beat — robot fails AND wins: Olympics crashes, kung-fu gone wrong, record sprints, absurd demos. Affectionate roast. Never mock injury victims (those stay in the serious lane).
